Chocolate Chip Cookies


Chocolate chip cookies are a simple but delicious treat for any occasion! Not only do they taste amazing, but they'll make your home smell like heaven while baking.

    Ingredients:

  • 1 cup salted butter - softened/melted
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 cup of brown sugar packed
  • 2 tsp of vanilla extract
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp of baking powder
  • 1 tsp sea salt
  • 2 cups choc chips

    Steps:

  1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees F (190 C). Line several sheets with parchment paper.

  2. In a bowl, combine flour, baking soda and powder, and salt. In another bowl combine your butter and sugar.

  3. After combining your butter & sugar, add in eggs and vanilla. Whisk this together for around 1 minute. Combine the dry ingredients afterwards.

  4. Add in your chocolate chips! I like to measure with my heart. After mixing them, let cool in fridge for 30 minutes to 2 hours. (optional)

  5. Once chilled, roll your dough into balls of whatever size you'd like. Remember they'll expand in oven, so the bigger the ball, the bigger the cookie. Line the baking sheets prepared from earlier. Place them with even spacing.

  6. Bake them for 8-10 minutes, you don't want them a dark brown when taking them out. When they're golden, remove them from the oven. After baking, let them cool for a bit. 5-10 minutes. Enjoy!
