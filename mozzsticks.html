Mozzarella Sticks

Mozzarella sticks are a staple appetizer. As a kid, I remember taking a bite and attempting to stretch the cheese out of my mouth. There cripsy exterior and melted cheese interior make this dish a hit among all ages.

ingredients

  • 2 beaten eggs
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 2 cups of breadcrumbs, italian or seasoned
  • 1/2 tsp garlic salt
  • 2/3 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/3 cup cornstarch
  • 2 cups oil for frying, or as needed
  • 1 package of mozzarella cheese sticks

Steps

  1. whisk eggs and water together. In a different bowl, combine your bread crumbs and garlic salt. In another bowl, whisk your flour and cornstarch. Begin heating your oil in saucepan to around 365 degrees F ( 185 degrees C).
