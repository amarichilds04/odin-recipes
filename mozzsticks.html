Mozzarella Sticks

Mozzarella sticks are a staple appetizer. As a kid, I remember taking a bite and attempting to stretch the cheese out of my mouth. There cripsy exterior and melted cheese interior make this dish a hit among all ages.

  1. 2 beaten eggs
  2. 1/4 cup water
  3. 2 cups of breadcrumbs, italian or seasoned
  4. 1/2 tsp garlic salt
  5. 2/3 cup all-purpose flour
  6. 1/3 cup cornstarch
  7. 2 cups oil for frying, or as needed
  8. 1 package of mozzarella cheese sticks
