Mozzarella Sticks

Mozzarella sticks are a staple appetizer. As a kid, I remember taking a bite and attempting to stretch the cheese out of my mouth. There cripsy exterior and melted cheese interior make this dish a hit among all ages.

Ingredients

  • 2 beaten eggs
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 2 cups of breadcrumbs, italian or seasoned
  • 1/2 tsp garlic salt
  • 2/3 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/3 cup cornstarch
  • 2 cups oil for frying, or as needed
  • 1 package of mozzarella cheese sticks

Steps

  1. Whisk eggs and water together. In a different bowl, combine your bread crumbs and garlic salt. In another bowl, whisk your flour and cornstarch.

  2. Take a mozzarella stick in and dip in the flour mixture, be sure to shake off the excess. Then, dip into the egg mixture, letting excess liquid drip off like you did with the flour. Dip the coated cheese into the breadcrumbs. After dipping into the breadcrumbs, dip into egg batter again and breadcrumbs one final time. Place on a baking sheet or plate when completed. Repeat the coating process for the remaining cheese sticks.

  3. Freeze your mozzarella sticks before cooking to ensure the coating stays on. Anywhere from 45 minutes to 2 hours should be enough!

  4. Begin heating your oil. Once its reached around 365 degrees f ( 185 degrees C) Begin placing sticks in the frying oil. Smaller batches is ideal so the oil maitains its temperature. Avoid overcrowding by cooking 3-4 at a time. They'll be crispy and golden-brown when done. They usually need 1-2 minutes in the oil before being done. Enjoy!
