Tuscan Chicken Pasta!!


Tuscan chicken pasta holds a special place in my heart. It's one of the first recipes I learned to cooked being moved out that I was actually proud of! I've cooked this for dates, friends, family, and its been a hit every single time. Not only is the prep easy to complete, but the flavors mesh so well together and bring restaurant level cuisine into your kitchen.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs of chicken breast
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 3 cloves garlic - minced
  • 1 cup sun-dried tomatoes - drained & chopped
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 cup of chicken broth
  • 2-3 cups of parmesan cheese - add more or less based on preference
  • 2-3 cups of baby spinach - add based on preference
  • 8 oz rigatoni, penne, or bowtie pasta

    • Seasonings:

    • Italian Seasoning
    • Salt & Pepper
    • Garlic Powder
    • Onion Powder
  1. Prepare chicken: Season chicken breasts with salt, pepper, italian , garlic powder, onion powder. Make sure both sides are seasoned well. Heat oil up on medium and cook chicken around 4 minutes per side. Remove when internal temperature is 165 and set to side. You can dice the chicken if you'd like.

  2. Add your butter to a pan, and set the heat to medium. After melted, add your garlic and let cook until fragrant (usually a minute). Add sundried tomatoes and italian seasoning.

  3. Add chicken broth and let heat up for 2-3 minutes. Add your spinach as the broth heats up in the mixture. While this is warming, boil water in a seperate pot and wait until you see prominent bubbles before adding your pasta. Follow directions on pasta package for proper time to cook till al dente. Then add your heavy cream gradually and whisk into pot with other ingredients. After you've added all your cream, add your parmesan gradually. Once the sauce becomes more creamier, add your chicken. Drain your pasta, and your pasta is ready to serve once the sauce is creamy to your liking!
